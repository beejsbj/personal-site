## A day you can shape

Dayshaper is a day planner built around a tactile 24-hour clay ring. Its sky follows the sun, with real moon and weather conditions giving the day a sense of place. The planner is designed to make time feel visible and workable.

Blocks can be moved, resized, reordered, and renamed as plans change. Each day has a shareable URL, and the installable, offline-capable PWA works without an account or signup.
