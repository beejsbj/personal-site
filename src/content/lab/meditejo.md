Meditejo is my personal meditation app for Android. I made it to keep practice material that was scattered across Dharma Seed, YouTube, and courses in one calm place.

It is a fork of [Medito](https://github.com/meditohq/medito-app), the open-source app by the Medito Foundation, and keeps Medito's player and timer. The bells and bowls are generated by synthesis, with no recorded samples. Home shows a different generated mark each day.
