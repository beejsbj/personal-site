<h3>Movie</h3>

<p>I watched the Batman, and this is the last section im putting in so im exausted. it was a visual feast. the visual direction was amazing. the camera choices inspired. the framing.</p>

<p>It wasnt a superhero movie as much as a detective movie. which yay!</p>

<p>I love though, how this movie explores Gotham, Batman and Bruce wayne. How Bruce Wayne not existing, as he traditionally is, affects the city, affects him and affects batman.</p>

<p>its interesting, in that it showcases how this Myth figure needs to be both, Bruce and Bats. and how both those sides of him are needed for gotham to function.</p>

<p>ive always considered superheroes to be modern myths that define our culture, like Hercules and Poseidon. we explore these characters through so many lenses.</p>

<p>Bruce/Batman himself is an emergent figure of a city. an urban city that itself is a capitalist metaorganism, and bruce himself is a product of the system. gotham itself is this myth figure for cities.</p>

<p>his parents killed by someone who the system fucked over, his parents who the system helped and is the reason why this orphan child was able to continue living. why he was able to turn into the batman.</p>

<p>I think my thoughts here need some more working on. but the linking of bruce, batman, cities, and capitalism is something i find fascinating. i wonder is the Sapiens book will give me more tools to work on this with.</p>
