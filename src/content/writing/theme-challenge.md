<h3>Intro</h3>

<p>Before I get started here outlining the process, know that its linearity betrays the experience and does not represent the chaos that is the <em>process</em>.</p>

<p>even while writing this, there is a tension between revealing my process authentically including my fears and anxieties and sanitizing it for you so I appear more competent.</p>

<h3>Goals of the Project</h3>

<p>This will be tough because my perception of what the goals were shifted through the project.</p>

<p>The way the project worked out was that we were given HTML/PHP to style. So it was a CSS/design challenge, ie, can you make a site look like it belonged in the space I was trying to emulate? In my case, it was to be a marijuana e-commerce website.</p>

<p>In addition to specifically focusing on the small details that sell the feel of professionalism and industry.</p>

<p>Initially, I assumed the idea was to create a design that looked like it belonged, but later after feedback, I realized that it was probably trying to copy and fit in as much as possible.</p>
