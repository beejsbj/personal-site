<p>after debating with myself between all the bootcampsI joined Design for the Web, dropped 10kUSD upfront for it.</p>

<p><em>WOO! DEBT!!</em></p>

<p>When i made my decision, i weighed the consequences of going into debt. and I decided, I chose to take that responsibility on. me and the family I come from, are not big risk takers or loaners or debts. Interest is considered <em>Haram </em>and that was always drilled into my head. its a whooole different paradigm.</p>

<p>but i needed to break out of that, I had to take this leap of faith, and even if it doesn’t pan out I wont regret it. because I <em><strong>will</strong></em> find value in the experience of it all, whether as a success or a failure. That is the kind of person i <em><strong>want</strong></em> to be, one who doesn’t <em>regret</em>, and the only way to do that is to find value in failure, because failure is inevitable and perfection is impossible.</p>

<p>My parents don’t believe in failure, its the typical Asian/Indian family that aren’t satisfied with anything other than an A++, and I naturally internalized those ideas too.</p>

<p>So im deciding to take those risky moves while i still have the privilege of being their kid instead of buying into their idea of failure and risk aversion. and i recognize this may not even register as a blip to others, which goes to show the crazy wide human experience is.</p>

<p>I knew i wouldn’t regret it, but i’m so glad i’m having so much fun with this. Theres a sense of direction to my days. everyday feels pleasant and joyous and as someone who’s been in the deeeeeeeeeeeeep hole of depression, theres a tangible difference between the experiences. and its always fascinating watching my mind flip between them. seeing the mental models shift, feeling the figurative visceral literal lights turn on and off.</p>

<p>I like Derek, I like that he genuinely gives a fuck about the students that come to him. it feels like a mentorship. it feels like I’m a blacksmith apprentice learning to smith websites.</p>

<p>I could probably be engaging with my classmates more but i don’t want to fuel that with the anxiety of “i’m not doing that” because that just puts uncomfortable pressure that leads to unnatural interactions.</p>

<p>my mind already jumps to comparisons, how they are amazing, having full professional jobs and doing this program on top. I’m eternally grateful for the life I’ve been given, I work to pay off the debt now that I’m in, and not much more. my family is a privilege, is a blessing.</p>

<p>the fact i get to see this mentorship and the debt to tackle as a <em><strong>challenge for 2023 </strong></em> is itself indicative of my headspace.</p>

<p>and i’m looking forward to the next 5 months!</p>
