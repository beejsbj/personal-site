<p>First of all, it’s a great story. Second its about Language and Cognition. so even better.</p>

<p>two things i’m really really into.</p>

<p>it feels like no one really talks about stuff related to it, like say, <strong>the first person internal experience</strong> of <em>feeling</em> the passage of time and aging.</p>

<p>No one talks about the experience of self when speaking different languages.I moved to Canada 6 years ago, and since then i’ve not gotten (or let myself) speak in other languages, i understand 2-3 more on top of english, and suck at speaking/writing them. <s>(always been better at listening/reading over speaking/writing. idkwhy🤷)</s></p>

<p>but a week or so ago i spoke a few words in Urdu while driving in my car alone.</p>

<p>and it felt weird…</p>

<p>i felt like i was a <em>different</em> person in that moment.</p>

<p><strong>that</strong> no one talks about that. i guess people take it for granted, or dont bother noticing it cause its not really practical. <em><strong>idk</strong></em>.</p>

<h5>This story had something of that going on in it.</h5>

<p>it deals with how cognition changes because of technology.</p>

<p>how the invention of writing affected oral cultures.</p>

<p>how the invention of AI-based Video recorded history changes your memories. reveals how fabricated your own sense of reality truly is. how <strong>we</strong> create narratives through reshaping pieces of our memories to support the current version of our <strong>self</strong>.</p>

<p>theres this moment in the book, where an individual from a writing culture starts teaching someone, an adult, writing,</p>

<h6>and he had to explain what a word is,,</h6>

<p>that <em>reaaallly</em> got me thinking about how this guy is weird for not knowing words when he is speaking them, not being able to notice what he is speaking.</p>

<p>but my brain chewed on it more and more and realized, to him all he is making is a smooth stream of sounds, not unlike <strong>music</strong>.</p>

<h5>it also got me thinking about web design.</h5>

<p>and how <em>that</em> in itself is language, a tool for communication.</p>

<p>where you have to setup pages to convey a certain feel,</p>

<p>transmit certain information, in a specific ways.</p>

<p>how me learning about how a web page works behind the scenes, and how <strong>i’m</strong> now able to glimpse the “<em>words</em>”, the “<em>divs</em>”.</p>

<p>I <strong>wonder</strong> how malleable cognition truly is. surely theres a cap, its evolved in a specific environment to do specific things.</p>

<p>I <strong>wonder</strong> about Musicians, or people who know <em>music</em>, and if they listen to music the way i listen to English, distinguishing distinct “words”.</p>

<p>I know after learning how to do counts for dancing, that im definitely able to distinguish the “<strong>beats</strong>” as different divs.</p>

<p><em>sigh…</em></p>

<p>the worst part is that even if i learn Music theory, my mind will change <s>slowly</s>, and <strong>adapt</strong> through the whole process. i wish i could just <em><strong>Know</strong></em> a whole language, so i can just compare and contrast the first person subjective experiences…</p>

<p>the qualia</p>
