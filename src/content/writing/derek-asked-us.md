<h4>and i replied…</h4>

<p><em>(quoted below with <strong>some</strong> editing)</em></p>

<blockquote><p>blog is a nebulous thing though the goodness of a blog piece would depend on the context of the blog and what its about.</p><p>So, theres writing itself, which i would define as how well you are able to convey your ideas, and communicate to your audience what is in your head. rather than stuff like grammar or wtv.</p><p>then that leads us into your audience, is it wide? then you might use unnuanced language, but by doing that you lose nuance you would have with a nuanced audience where you could use more nuanced language to convey nuanced ideas with more nuance.</p><p>and so finally your audience is based on what your goals are;</p><p>are you trying to monetize the blog? cast a wide net.</p><p>you don’t care about money and are doing this to find like minded people to talk about cool stuff you share? go narrower.</p><p>or perhaps it’s not even about an audience and functions more like a public journal. something that gives <em>you</em> <strong>you</strong> a voice.</p><p>TLDR: the framework is dependant on you and your goals.</p></blockquote>
