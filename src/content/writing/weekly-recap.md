<h3>Personal</h3>

<h4>Movies</h4>

<p>Alas, i’ve not watched anything yet. i’m trying to solve this like i solved my reading issue. my biggest problems come from the fact i get overwhelmed by analysis paralysis.</p>

<p>The way i solved that with books is by literally just deciding in advanced. so i have 3 queues, and each has like 10+ books, so i don’t have to think about it for a while, and when i do, ill be in a different “decide” headspace rather than “i need to read something now but cant decide aaaaahh”</p>

<p>so i did implement queues for movies. and setup dedicated days for each queue. now i just need to execute. Im trying to get through like IMDBs top 250. like i’m guaranteed to like most things on there…surely.</p>

<p>the plan is to watch a movie once a week, so its a project that would take years to complete. sounds fun! but i had a glimpse of this experience before, for like twoish weeks. i just love stories, and i love how, <em>WHOLEY experiences</em> movies are. they are like landmarks in time and my days.</p>

<p>They tinge my day with their color and i love having that color through that day <strong>and</strong> to look back on.</p>

<p><em>Infact an idea for making a website comes from these queues, and my own analysis paralysis.</em></p>

<p>The way it would work it that the list/queue would be tied to a day, id be able to go onto the site and it’d just show me that day’s movie and nothing else. and then if i want to, i can scroll up to see the stuff ive watched. but nothing into the future.</p>

<p>that seems a more backend-y thing than front endy. but cool fading, and posters, and colors extraction from those posters is brewing in my noggin.</p>
