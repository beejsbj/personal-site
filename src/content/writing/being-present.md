<h2><em>breathe,,,</em></h2>

<p>Being present in the moment. Being mindful of the moment. letting the moment wholly envelope you and immersing yourself in it.</p>

<p>it’s something i know is incredibly power. You get to, in that moment you decide to notice the now, feel your whole body and the universe. Such a simple way to ground yourself.</p>

<p>so so simple, yet so hard to remember to do.</p>

<p>It’s so easy to get lost in the world, in your mind, in others’ minds. in the past and the future. I know that is most definitely true for myself.</p>

<h3><em>BreAthE.</em></h3>

<p>I always find myself rushing to get to the next moment, to the next scheduled thing, to the next next next.. thing. even if i really want to do the thing I’m currently at the moment. caused by this dread of the time ticking.</p>

<p>I do assignments, and rush to get them “done”. i read a book and rush to get it “finished”. I watch a movie and rush to check it off the list.</p>

<p>I play a game and can’t sit still, I download walkthroughs to get them “done” instead of enjoying each moment of being in that world that i have the privilege to experience. each challenge i come across. each character i get to meet. and in that rush to the finish line i get burnt.</p>

<p>I’m reminded of <em>Tick Tick.. Boom!</em> and <em>Hamilton</em>, both of the protagonists die young. both feeling the pressure of time, why do you write like you’re running out of time. both in a hurry to leave a legacy.</p>

<h4><em><strong>BREATHE</strong></em></h4>

<p>I wish i could just Be. The waves of my mind and the world are so strong. I’m on my way there, a future me will get to live the way i want to, the way i need to. but I’m not there yet.</p>

<p>That’s okay, i’ll be gone when the next moment arrives but i’ll pave the way for you. for us.</p>
