# Personal Site

The personal site presents Burooj's work as an apparently normal portfolio that is actually housed inside a stranger interactive prism structure.

## Language

**Project**:
A lasting piece of Burooj's work with a home of its own. A project may have a detail page and multiple updates over time; experiments may graduate into projects.
_Avoid_: update, activity event

**Update**:
One dated entry in the Labbook: something Burooj made, changed, or published. It may refer to a project or experiment and may carry an artifact, but does not require a full case study.
_Avoid_: project, slice, raw commit log

**Labbook**:
The complete, dated record of updates, like a lab notebook or a game journal. It holds everything that has been published; the ticker shows only its highlights.
_Avoid_: journal, feed, changelog

**Headline**:
A short, possibly playful line written about one or more updates for the ticker. A headline always points back to the updates it describes.
_Avoid_: update, announcement

**Lab**:
Burooj's workshop, seen through a window: a room the visitor steps into to watch work in progress, live and in history. Private things stay visibly redacted rather than hidden.
_Avoid_: cockpit, dashboard, experiments list

**bjslab**:
Burooj's home lab, the real place the Lab is a window into. It feeds the room rather than appearing as an entry in it.
_Avoid_: home server project, lab entry

**Bench**:
The part of the Lab holding experiments being worked on now.
_Avoid_: active list, in-progress status

**Shelf**:
The part of the Lab holding experiments that have gone quiet, plus the archive of older work.
_Avoid_: graveyard, archived status

**Ticker**:
The ambient, always-moving strip of headlines and live presence. It shows what is highlighted, not the full record.
_Avoid_: feed, activity log

**Experiment**:
Exploratory work shown in the Lab, from a one-off sketch to a long-running line of tries. An experiment is a showcase of trying things: it may point straight to where it lives (a Pen, a demo, a deck) or tell its making as a short case study. It may graduate or go quiet.
_Avoid_: thread, lab item

**Graduation**:
The moment an experiment becomes a dedicated push and gains a project of its own.
_Avoid_: promotion, launch

**Prism Portfolio**:
The site experience where a normal portfolio is contained within a prism-like spatial frame that can later be exited, traversed, and re-entered through different lenses.
_Avoid_: 3D portfolio, cube demo, special mode

**Normal Portfolio**:
The straightforward portfolio surface that visitors first believe they are using: projects, writing, about, resume, and related professional material.
_Avoid_: fallback site, boring site

**Curiosity Reveal**:
The first hint that the normal portfolio is inside a stranger prism structure, shown only after the visitor lingers, explores, or otherwise signals curiosity.
_Avoid_: timed reveal, forced intro, onboarding prompt

**Prism Lure**:
An alive, catchable affordance from the prism that tempts the visitor to exit the normal portfolio and discover the surrounding structure. It is playfully evasive but generous enough to be caught before it becomes frustrating.
_Avoid_: seeker, golden snitch, exit button

**Prism Exit**:
The moment the camera pulls back from the normal portfolio and reveals that the portfolio is a surface within the prism. The portfolio remains intact rather than being torn, discarded, or exposed as fake: the face you pull out of is the live page itself, mid-whatever it was doing, and stepping back in returns it exactly as it was.
_Avoid_: page break, portal tear, escape sequence

**Settled Arrival**:
Arriving on a page that is already on screen: a prism face, or a lens stepped into from the prism. It lands at rest, without the entrance a real first arrival plays.
_Avoid_: skip intro, no-animation mode

**Face Travel**:
Movement outside the prism from one legible face to another, with each face acting as a distinct lens on the normal portfolio. Travel is fixed and deliberate rather than free flight.
_Avoid_: free roam, fly mode, open navigation

**Prism Face**:
One of the five complete skins that surround the normal portfolio within the pentagonal prism. Each face is a real visual treatment of the same portfolio, not a separate story world or content mode. The faces are Daylight, Calling Card, Cut Paper, Back Page and Hion.
_Avoid_: demo side, filler theme, optional face

**Lens**:
A visual skin for the same normal portfolio structure. A lens can change theme, typography, color, layout feel, motion, filters, and mood, but it does not create a separate portfolio or content model.
_Avoid_: content mode, separate site, alternate portfolio

**Refraction**:
The idea behind the prism: one body of work goes in as white light and comes out as five colours. Every lens is the same portfolio bent differently, never a different portfolio.
_Avoid_: variant, alternate site

**Lens Name**:
An internal handle for building and discussing a lens. Lens names may appear softly in the experience, but they are not presented as a theme-picker taxonomy.
_Avoid_: theme label, menu category, mode title

**First Journey**:
The visitor's first guided movement through the prism. It uses a planned sequence so the prism structure is legible before later revisiting becomes more open.
_Avoid_: forced tour, linear lock, onboarding

**Revisiting**:
Later movement through known prism faces after the first journey has made the structure understandable. Revisiting can be freer than the first journey.
_Avoid_: replay, theme picker, level select

**Default Lens**:
The first lens, Daylight: the default portfolio skin that reads as normal before the prism reveals itself.
_Avoid_: Plainface, boring mode, control page
